Groups

A group shows one client, brand or engagement at a time; choose it with the switcher in the sidebar. Every root domain is a group of its own until you put it in a named group. A domain includes all hostnames under it; an IP or CIDR range includes the IPs inside it. IPs your hostnames point to join their group, except CDN and shared-hosting addresses.
